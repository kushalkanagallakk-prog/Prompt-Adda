Privacy Policy for Prompt Adda

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Prompt Adda respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and the choices available to you when using the Prompt Adda application.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in using Google, Prompt Adda may receive basic account information such as your name, email address, profile photo, account identifier, and authentication information required to maintain your account.

The app may also store information associated with your activity, including saved favourites, comments, replies, app usage counters, account activity timestamps, and other features that you choose to use.

2. Firebase Services

Prompt Adda uses Google Firebase services including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for authentication, account-related features, storing favourites, comments, replies, application content, and related data.

Firebase may process technical information such as device information, IP address, application information, diagnostics, and other data as described in Google's privacy documentation.

3. Advertising

Prompt Adda uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device or advertising identifiers, IP address, device and app information, advertising interactions, and related information for advertising, fraud prevention, measurement, and other advertising purposes.

Where required by applicable law, Prompt Adda uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent before using personal data for personalised advertising. Users may be shown personalised or non-personalised advertising depending on their consent choices and applicable regulations.

4. Images and Media

Prompt Adda may use image hosting and delivery services such as Cloudinary for application content. Camera or media access is only used when an applicable feature requires the user or authorised administrator to select or capture media.

5. How We Use Information

Information is used to provide and maintain Prompt Adda features, authenticate users, save user preferences and favourites, enable comments and replies, improve application functionality, maintain security, prevent abuse, and display advertising.

6. Third-Party Services

Prompt Adda may use services provided by:

  • Google Firebase
  • Google Sign-In
  • Google AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform
  • Cloudinary

These third-party services may process data according to their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Data Sharing

Prompt Adda does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the application, provide authentication, cloud storage, advertising, analytics, security, and related functionality.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the application's services, maintain account functionality, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and enforce agreements.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Where required, users can manage advertising consent choices through the privacy options provided in Prompt Adda.

Users may also request information about or deletion of applicable personal data by contacting us.

10. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Prompt Adda features, services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
